What Lulu Kaya means

“Lulu Kaya” is a blend of beautiful language influences.

Lulu comes from Arabic and Swahili, meaning a pearl: something precious, rare and beautiful. Kaya means home across Africa: from the Mijikenda of the Kenyan coast to South Africa, where ikhaya is home. A place of belonging and refuge.

Bring the two together and Lulu Kaya means “where beauty finds its home.”

That is exactly what our fragrances are about: helping you carry your confidence, your identity and your presence every day.

It’s not just perfume. It’s your identity.

Know your perfume

What is in the bottle, and how it smells.

Every perfume is built in three layers called notes. You smell them one after another as the oil warms on your skin.

1 · First impression Top notes The first thing you smell when you open the bottle or put it on. Light, bright and fresh. Think of
  • Lemon
  • Bergamot
  • Orange
  • Mint
  • Fresh fruit
Tip: This is what you notice first on a tester strip.
2 · The character Heart notes The body of the perfume: what it really smells like once it settles on your skin. Think of
  • Rose
  • Jasmine
  • Spices
  • Lavender
  • Ripe fruit
Tip: Put a little on your wrist and give it a moment before you decide.
3 · The lasting memory Base notes The deep, rich notes that stay on your skin and clothes. Think of
  • Vanilla
  • Musk
  • Woods
  • Amber
  • Oud
Tip: This is what people remember when you walk past.

How to mix

  • Start with two scents. Add a third only when you know the first two work.
  • Pair something fresh or fruity with something sweet, such as citrus with vanilla.
  • Musk softens almost anything. Add it to a floral to make it smoother.
  • Oud and amber are strong. Balance them with rose or vanilla.

How to wear perfume oil

  • Dab on warm spots: wrists, neck and behind the ears.
  • Use a little. Oil is concentrated, so one or two dabs are enough.
  • Let it settle for a few minutes before judging the scent. The heart and base take time to show.

Good to know

Questions customers ask.

How long does the perfume last?

Our perfume oils last for more than 24 hours.

Can I return a perfume?

No. We do not accept returns, so all sales are final. Smell the scent at our shop first, or start with a 3 ml bottle.

How do I pay?

With MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money, using the merchant codes shown at checkout. The merchant name you will see is ADISI YA AFRIKA. Pay first, then send your order on WhatsApp with a screenshot of the payment message.

Do you deliver?

Yes. In Kampala we send by SafeBoda and you pay the rider directly when your order arrives. Outside Kampala we send by bus and you pay the delivery charge. The checkout shows an estimate for your area.

Can I mix scents?

Yes, from a 20 ml bottle upwards. A mixed bottle costs the same as any bottle of that size.

How do loyalty points work?

Every UGX 50,000 you spend at retail earns 1 point. For wholesale, every UGX 72,000 earns 1 point. Your spending adds up across orders, and delivery charges do not count. With 7 points you get a free 20 ml bottle in the scent of your choice. To redeem, follow us on Instagram or TikTok. We keep your points on your WhatsApp number and tell you your balance with each receipt. To check it any time, message us on WhatsApp.
